## Seating and groups

We take reservations; call (718) 606-8026. Good for kids, with a kids' menu.

## Ordering ahead and delivery

[Order ahead online](https://tasteofitaly.direct-ordering.com) and pick up at the restaurant. We deliver within about 1 miles for a $3 fee, with a $12 minimum. [Order online](https://tasteofitaly.direct-ordering.com) or call (718) 606-8026.

## Amenities and payment

You'll find a wheelchair-accessible entrance. We don't have outdoor seating. We accept credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.

## What to order

**Linguine Calamari**: The Linguine Calamari costs $20.95. It's made with tender calamari rings served over linguine in your choice of red or white sauce, mild sweet or fiery hot and a true taste of the Italian coast.

**Taste of Italy Special**: The Taste of Italy Special costs $12.95. It's made with our signature loaded pie topped with sweet sausage, pepperoni, fresh mushrooms, onions, bell peppers and and a generous layer of extra mozzarella.

## About us

We’re the kind of neighborhood place built for weeknight dinners, birthday tables, quick slices, and big family orders. Our menu brings together classic pizza, specialty pies, pasta, hot heroes, wings, salads, dinners, and family combos. [Read our story](/about/).

## Where to find us

Taste of Italy is at 40-05 Junction Blvd, Queens, NY. We're open Mon–Sun 11 AM to 10 PM.
