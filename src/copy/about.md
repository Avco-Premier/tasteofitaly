We’re the kind of neighborhood place built for weeknight dinners, birthday tables, quick slices, and big family orders.

Our menu brings together classic pizza, specialty pies, pasta, hot heroes, wings, salads, dinners, and family combos. It’s a broad neighborhood menu with something for everyone at the table.

Whether you’re stopping in, feeding the family, planning lunch for the office, or ordering delivery, our goal is simple: make the meal easy and make it satisfying.

## Before you visit

You'll find a wheelchair-accessible entrance. We don't have outdoor seating. We accept credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.

## Where to find us

Taste of Italy is at 40-05 Junction Blvd, Queens, NY. We're open Mon–Sun 11 AM to 10 PM.

## What we serve

The full [menu](/menu/) covers menu family special combo ($10.95–$29.95), drinks ($1.50–$5.99), gourmet Italian pizza ($12.95–$24.95), chef salads ($12.95–$15.95), buffalo chicken wings ($14.95–$17.95), special Italian pasta ($13.95–$21.95), Italian hot heros ($9.95–$11.95), burgers ($9.95–$14.95), Taste Of Italy special dinner ($17.95–$21.95), Italian wraps ($9.95–$11.95), chef’s speciality ($13.95–$20.95), lunch specials every day ($15.95), mini gourmet pizzas ($8.95–$11.95) and kids menu ($7.95–$11.95).

## Signature dishes

**Linguine Calamari**: The Linguine Calamari costs $20.95. It's made with tender calamari rings served over linguine in your choice of red or white sauce, mild sweet or fiery hot and a true taste of the Italian coast.

**Taste of Italy Special**: The Taste of Italy Special costs $12.95. It's made with our signature loaded pie topped with sweet sausage, pepperoni, fresh mushrooms, onions, bell peppers and and a generous layer of extra mozzarella.

**8 Buffalo Wings**: The 8 Buffalo Wings costs $14.95. It's made with 8 fresh jumbo chicken wings with crisp celery and carrots, hot, BBQ and or spicy BBQ., served with blue cheese or ranch dressing. Choose your sauce: medium.
